Calculation Formula

Manufacturing LT = (Base LT + Drawing Breakdown LT + Customer Difficulty Buffer + Special Treatment Buffer) × Quantity Factor

ETD = Manufacturing Start Date + Total Manufacturing LT

Version 2.1. Calendar days are used for ETD calculation. CCT above 1,000 and Level 5 require advance discussion.
